# Code of conduct

TF3 Companion is a small, friendly project for people who enjoy Transport Fever 3 and building tools around it. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space that represents the project is expected to follow these rules.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith and ask for clarification before assuming the worst.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; credit other people's work.
- Keep reports and examples free of other people's private data: no real save files, account identifiers, personal paths or contact details.
- Use English in the repository so every contributor can follow the discussion.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, sexualised language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, sustained disruption of discussions, and any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation by anyone whose behaviour they judge harmful. They will explain the decision when it is safe to do so.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to alexandrevl@gmail.com with the subject `TF3 Companion conduct`. Reports are handled in confidence, and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent. Maintainers will acknowledge a report within 7 days and decide on a response that is proportionate: usually a private reminder, then a warning, then a temporary or permanent restriction.

Security vulnerabilities are not handled here; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
