## 1. Town rating formulas (happiness, on-time delivery)

If `sens <= 0` the rating is 1.0 (rating disabled). prog:93-101. Relevance high. Home mechanics.md M2 (replace the vague "rescaled by sensitivity"). - [CODE] **On-time delivery rating**: `delivered = max(10, delivered)`; `frac = 1 − late/delivered`; same `low`/`high`/clamp as above with the cargo sensitivity; `sens <= 0` → 1.0. prog:103-111. Relevance high. Home mechanics.md M2/M12. Status CONFIRMS-EXISTING (mechanics.md:125 "minimum denominator of 10 items") and NEW (rescale formula).
